3. Who can buy

You must be at least 18 years old to reserve or buy a puppy. Because our puppies' welfare comes first, we may decline a reservation if we're not confident a puppy will have a safe and suitable home.

4. Reservations and deposits

You can ask to reserve a puppy by phone or WhatsApp. A reservation is only confirmed when we confirm it to you in writing, usually by WhatsApp message. Your written confirmation will set out:

  • the puppy you're reserving and its price;
  • any deposit required, and whether and when it can be refunded;

  • the details of any payment plan we've agreed.

Your written confirmation forms part of your agreement with us. If anything in it differs from these terms, the written confirmation applies.

5. Prices, payment and payment plans

Prices on our website are in US dollars and are per puppy. The price confirmed in your written reservation is the price you pay, even if the website price changes afterwards. If we make an obvious mistake in a price, we'll tell you before you pay and you can choose whether to go ahead.

Unless we've agreed a payment plan with you, the full price must be paid before or when your puppy is handed over to you. Any delivery fee will be agreed with you in advance.

Where we offer financing or a payment plan, its amounts, due dates, when your puppy can go home, and what happens if a payment is missed will all be set out in writing before you commit.

6. Health and vaccinations

Every puppy is health-checked and receives the vaccinations appropriate for its age before it goes home, and we'll share the health and vaccination information we hold for your puppy.

Puppies need further vaccinations and regular care as they grow. We strongly recommend you take your puppy to a vet within a few days of it arriving home, and follow your vet's advice on vaccinations, deworming and check-ups.

Puppies are living animals. We breed carefully, but we can't guarantee a dog's future health, size or temperament. If we offer a health guarantee for your puppy, its terms will be set out in your written confirmation. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under consumer protection law.

If your puppy seems unwell after arriving home, take it to a vet promptly and contact us as soon as you can.

7. Delivery

We offer home delivery across the country. We'll agree the delivery date, approximate time and any fee with you in advance. An adult must be present to receive the puppy.

Your puppy's safety comes first. If your puppy is unwell, or travel conditions aren't safe, we may postpone delivery and will agree a new date with you. Responsibility for your puppy passes to you when it is handed over.
