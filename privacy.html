Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Clint Kennels ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or contact us about a puppy, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

At a glance

  • Our website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We only collect what you choose to share when you call, WhatsApp or message us.
  • We use it to help you find, reserve and receive your puppy, and nothing else.
  • We never sell or rent your personal information.

1. Information we collect

When you visit our website

Our website has no sign-up forms, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers. Like every website, the service that hosts it may automatically record basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of your visit, to keep the site secure and working.

Some puppy photos on the site are loaded from Unsplash, an image service. When those images load, Unsplash receives your IP address and browser information, as it would for any image on the web.

When you contact us

When you call us, message us on WhatsApp or send us a message on Instagram or Facebook, we receive:

  • your name and phone number, and your WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook profile name and picture;
  • the messages, photos and voice notes you send us;
  • details you share about the puppy you're looking for and your home, so we can help you choose the right match.

When you reserve or buy a puppy

  • your delivery address and any delivery instructions;
  • payment records, such as the amount, date, payment method and reference. We do not store full bank card details;
  • if you choose a payment plan, the details needed to set it up and keep track of payments;
  • new-owner details we record with your puppy's health and vaccination information.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • answer your questions and help you choose a puppy;
  • reserve your puppy and confirm the details of your purchase;
  • take payments and manage any payment plan you've agreed with us;
  • arrange delivery of your puppy to your home;
  • give you your puppy's health and vaccination information;
  • check in on how your puppy is settling in and answer aftercare questions;
  • keep the business and tax records the law requires;
  • protect the welfare of our dogs and prevent fraud.

We won't send you marketing messages unless you ask us to, for example to be told when a new litter arrives. You can tell us to stop at any time.

3. Why we're allowed to use it

We only use your personal information when we have a valid reason under data protection law:

  • To take steps you've asked for or to fulfil our agreement with you, such as reserving, selling and delivering your puppy.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping financial records.
  • For our legitimate interests, such as running our business, keeping records of which home each puppy went to, and preventing fraud, as long as those interests don't override your rights.
  • With your consent, for example to send you news about upcoming litters. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it when needed to look after you and your puppy:

  • Delivery partners: your name, phone number and address, so your puppy can be delivered safely.
  • Veterinarians: owner details where needed for your puppy's health records.
  • Banks and mobile money providers: to process your payments.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: such as our accountant, or a court or government body when the law requires it.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or your phone network, those companies also process your messages and calls under their own privacy policies.

5. Third-party services and links

Our website links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook, which are run by Meta, and loads some images from Unsplash. These services have their own privacy policies, and we're not responsible for how they handle your information. We encourage you to read their policies before using them.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only as long as we need them to help you. If you buy a puppy, we keep a record of the sale, including who the puppy went to, its health and vaccination information, and payment records, for as long as the law requires us to keep business records, and so we can help with any later questions about your dog. When we no longer need your information, we delete it or make sure it can no longer identify you.

7. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access. Only the people who need it to look after you and your puppy can see it. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we'll tell you promptly if a problem affects your information.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that's wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we have to keep it by law;
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose, including marketing;
  • withdraw any consent you've given.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We'll respond as soon as we can and within the time the law allows.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please talk to us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority. In Zimbabwe, that is the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ).

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll make that clear on our website.
